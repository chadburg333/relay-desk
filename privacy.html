Privacy policy

Relay Desk · Updated November 3, 2026

The short version

Relay Desk has no servers of its own. Your tasks, calendar and email stay on your devices, in your own iCloud, and in your own Google account. Nothing is collected, sold or shared.

Your tasks and projects

Tasks, projects and notes are saved on your device and synced between your own devices through your private iCloud database. The developer can't see them.

Google Calendar and Gmail

When you connect a Google account, Relay Desk talks to Google directly from your device to:

  • show your calendars and events, and change an event only after you confirm it;
  • show your mail, and archive, star, mark read or unread, and send replies or new messages you write.

The sign-in is kept in your device's Keychain. Copies of events and mail are kept on that device only, to show them quickly. Disconnecting Google in Settings removes the sign-in. Relay Desk never deletes mail.

Relay Desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to show it to you and act on your instructions in the app. It is not transferred to anyone, used for advertising, or read by people.

Analytics and tracking

None. Relay Desk has no analytics, ads or trackers.
